## Scope

The 100 Saudi PPF keywords are distributed across the homepage, English and Arabic service pages, English and Arabic editorial pages, and Google Business Profile posts. The plan deliberately avoids placing every keyword on one page. Each page should use one primary phrase, two to four closely related phrases, and natural service language.

## GBP posting cadence

Publish one useful post weekly for twelve weeks. Rotate between service education, care guidance, before-and-after media that represents real completed work, and booking prompts. Do not publish fabricated reviews, invented customer names, artificial ratings, or stock “customer result” claims. Every post should link to the closest relevant landing page and use a real image or video from the studio.

## Measurement and governance

Track GBP views, website clicks, calls, direction requests, and booked consultations separately from organic search. Review Search Console queries monthly, then refine page copy only where it improves clarity and user intent. Keep Arabic copy natively written, use Arabic page titles and headings, and maintain one canonical URL per page with the Arabic and English alternates pointing to real routes.
